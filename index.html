Privacy Policy

Ledgerly
Last updated: November 15, 2026

Ledgerly is a local-first personal finance app designed to help you track spending, income, accounts, budgets, and categories. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and what happens when you choose optional cloud backup features.

Important: Ledgerly does not collect, store, or access users' financial records, transaction history, account details, or personal budget data on our servers. Your financial data is stored locally on your device unless you choose to create an encrypted Google Drive backup. We do not sell your personal information. We only process data when needed to provide the app’s core features, Google account backup/restore, or in-app advertising.

1. Information we collect

App data stored on your device

Ledgerly stores financial data locally on the device you use to access the app. We do not store your transaction records, account history, budgets, or other financial data on our own servers. This may include:

  • Account balances and account names
  • Income and expense records
  • Categories, budgets, and custom settings
  • App preferences and local configuration

This local app data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up to Google Drive or delete it from the app.

Google account information

If you choose to sign in with Google and enable backup features, Ledgerly may access the information needed to use Google Sign-In and Google Drive, including:

  • Basic Google account identity information required for sign-in
  • Google Drive access needed to create or retrieve backup files
  • Metadata for the backup file stored in your own Google Drive account

This access is only used when you explicitly choose to link your Google account or use the backup flow.

Backup data

When you use the Google Drive backup feature, Ledgerly prepares a backup of your local app data. The app encrypts the backup on the device before upload. The encryption key is kept locally on the device and is not sent to our servers.

The encrypted backup may be stored in a visible folder named “Ledgerly Backups” in your Google Drive account. The app may also read and restore that backup later if you choose to restore from Google Drive.

Advertising and device information

Ledgerly uses Google AdMob to display ads in some parts of the app. Google may process advertisement-related information such as:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • Device type and operating system information
  • Approximate location data for ad serving
  • Usage data required to provide and measure ads

These advertising identifiers are managed by Google’s advertising services and are subject to Google’s own privacy policies. Ledgerly does not use your financial records for ad targeting.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the core finance tracking features of Ledgerly
  • Store and restore your data locally on your device
  • Enable optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup/restore
  • Improve app reliability and basic service quality
  • Display ads through Google AdMob

We do not use your financial records for advertising or profiling.

3. Data storage and retention

Most Ledgerly data is stored locally on the device where the app is installed. We do not maintain a separate cloud database for your everyday financial records unless you choose to create a Google Drive backup.

Google Drive backups remain in your own Google Drive account until you delete the backup file or sign out of the Google account connection from the app. Local app data remains on the device until you remove it from the app or uninstall the app.

We keep app data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required for legal obligations.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data only in the following limited situations:

  • With Google services when you enable Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup/restore
  • With Google AdMob for advertising and ad measurement purposes
  • When required by law or valid legal process

We do not transmit your encryption key to our servers. The backup file itself is encrypted before it is uploaded to Google Drive.

5. Your choices and controls

You can control whether Ledgerly uses Google Drive backup features:

  • Use the app without linking a Google account
  • Sign in with Google only when you want to create or restore backups
  • Sign out from Google in the app whenever you want to disconnect the account link
  • Delete backup files from your Google Drive account directly if you no longer want them stored there

Signing out of Google does not delete your local app data. It only disconnects the app from your Google account and stops Google Drive access for the backup feature.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely risk-free, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored on a device or in a third-party cloud service such as Google Drive.

For Google Drive backups, the app encrypts the data locally before upload to help protect the contents of the backup file while it is stored in your Google Drive.

7. General audience

Ledgerly is intended for general users and is not limited to children under 13. We do not target the app to children specifically, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that would be inconsistent with responsible digital services practices.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
